FAQ

Good questions,
straight answers.

How long does a typical project take?

A landing page usually ships within a week. Full business websites and chatbot projects typically take 1–3 weeks depending on scope. You'll get a realistic timeline in the proposal before any work starts.

How do payments work?

Milestone-based: a deposit to begin, the balance at approval or launch. Every quote is fixed and in writing — no surprise invoices at the end.

Do you work with businesses outside India?

Absolutely. Most of our clients are in the USA, UK and Australia. Everything is handled remotely — email, WhatsApp and scheduled calls in your time zone, with secure international payment options.

What happens after launch?

Every project includes post-launch support. Beyond that, we offer monthly care plans covering hosting, backups, updates, monitoring and small changes — so your site never goes stale.
